# About the Author

Mike MacCana creates Solana content at Quicknode, where he maintains the most comprehensive open library of tested Solana programs: the companion repository this book is built on. He previously ran developer education at the Solana Foundation, where he created a training program taught to hundreds of students from blockchain clubs in Romania to Ivy League universities, and co-created and presented the Solana Foundation Developer Bootcamp, watched 350,000 times. He is a top-ten contributor on Solana StackExchange, created the most popular high-level library for web3.js, and is a two-time prize winner at the Solana Sandstorm hackathon.

Before Solana, Mike worked at financial giants including Man Group, Credit Suisse, and CMC Invest, and at Google, Microsoft, and Red Hat. He founded CertSimple, used by Buzzfeed, Monzo and most London fintechs, and created the python-docx library, which Microsoft uses in its own AI tools. He is in the top 0.1% of developers on Stack Overflow and has spoken at Solana Breakpoint and Accelerate. He lives in New York City.
