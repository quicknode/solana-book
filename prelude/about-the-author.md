# About the Author

Mike MacCana creates Solana content at Quicknode, where he maintains the most comprehensive open library of tested Solana programs: the companion repository this book is built on. Before Quicknode, he ran developer education at the Solana Foundation, built a training program used from student blockchain clubs in Romania to Ivy League universities in the US, and co-created and presented the Solana Foundation Developer Bootcamp. The bootcamp videos have been watched about 350,000 times. He is a top-ten contributor on Solana Stack Exchange and in the top 0.1% on Stack Overflow. He created multiple popular Solana libraries, including solana-helpers, and is a two-time prize winner at the Solana Sandstorm hackathon.

Before Solana, Mike worked at large financial firms including Man Group, Credit Suisse, and CMC Invest, and at Google, Microsoft, and Red Hat. He founded CertSimple, used by Buzzfeed, Monzo and most London fintechs, and created the python-docx library, which Microsoft uses in its own AI tools. He was the first product engineer at Humanloop, now part of Anthropic. He has spoken at Solana Breakpoint, Accelerate, and NodeConf. He lives in New York City and is on X as @mikemaccana.
