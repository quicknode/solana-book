# About the Author

Mike MacCana creates Solana content at Quicknode, where he maintains the most comprehensive open library of tested Solana programs: the companion repository this book is built on. Before Quicknode, he ran developer education at the Solana Foundation, where he created a four-day training program that has been taught to Ivy League universities, and maintained developer training that was the highest-retention content on the Foundation's website. He co-created and presented the Solana Foundation Developer Bootcamp, watched about 330,000 times on YouTube. Before the Foundation, he won two prizes at the Solana Sandstorm hackathon for Portal Payments, which showed a recipient's identity before funds were sent and recovered wallets without a passphrase. He is a top-ten contributor on Solana Stack Exchange and among the top 1,000 of Stack Overflow's roughly 31 million users. He is the author of solana-helpers and Kite, two Solana libraries downloaded more than 30,000 times a week.

Before Solana, Mike worked at large financial firms including Man Group, Credit Suisse, and CMC Invest, and at Google, Microsoft, and Red Hat. He founded CertSimple, used by Buzzfeed, Monzo and most London fintechs, and created the python-docx library, which Microsoft uses in its own AI tools. He was the first product engineer at Humanloop, now part of Anthropic. He has spoken at Solana Breakpoint, Solana Accelerate, and Digicert's cryptography forum. He lives in New York City and is on X as @mikemaccana.
