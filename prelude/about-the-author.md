# About the Author

Mike MacCana creates Solana content at Quicknode, where he maintains the most comprehensive open library of tested Solana programs: the companion repository this book is built on. Before Quicknode, he ran developer education at the Solana Foundation, where the developer courses he maintained were the highest-retention content on the Foundation's website. He also created a separate four-day training program, which has been taught to Ivy League universities. He co-created and presented the Solana Foundation Developer Bootcamp, watched about 350,000 times on YouTube. He is a top-ten contributor on Solana Stack Exchange and in the top 0.1% on Stack Overflow. He created solana-helpers and Kite, Solana TypeScript libraries downloaded more than 30,000 times a week. His two prizes at the Solana Sandstorm hackathon were for Portal Payments, which showed a recipient's identity before funds were sent and recovered wallets without a passphrase.

Before Solana, Mike worked at large financial firms including Man Group, Credit Suisse, and CMC Invest, and at Google, Microsoft, and Red Hat. He founded CertSimple, used by Buzzfeed, Monzo and most London fintechs, and created the python-docx library, which Microsoft uses in its own AI tools. He was the first product engineer at Humanloop, now part of Anthropic. He has spoken at Solana Breakpoint, Solana Accelerate, and Digicert's cryptography forum. He lives in New York City and is on X as @mikemaccana.
