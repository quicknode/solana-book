# About the Technical Reviewers

This page is reserved for you. If you are reading this advance copy, you are one of the technical reviewers this section will name: as reviews land, each reviewer is credited here with their name, title, and project (tell us if you would rather not be). Corrections that land in the manuscript are additionally credited in the Acknowledgements.

The reviewer guide sent with your invitation covers what feedback matters most and how to file it.
