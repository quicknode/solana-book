# About Quicknode

Quicknode is a Y Combinator-backed company founded in 2017, and one of the first blockchain infrastructure companies in the world. In 2021 it became the first commercial RPC provider to support Solana. It now provides low-latency infrastructure to major banks, fintechs, and card providers, and to many of the best-known teams on Solana.

Quicknode's Solana account on X is @QuicknodeSolana.
