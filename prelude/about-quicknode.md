# About Quicknode

Quicknode s a YCombinator backed startup that pioneered the concept of blockchain RPC providers in 2017. In 2021 it was the first SaaS RPC provider to support Solanam, and now provides low latency blockchain infrastructure across the world to major banks, fintechs and card providers as well a number of the most well known teams in Solana. 

Quicknode maintains a dedicated Solana account on X at @QuicknodeSolana
