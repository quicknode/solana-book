# Reviewing This Book

Thank you for reviewing *Building Financial Software on Solana*. This page is everything you need.

## What to read

You have a PDF advance copy and read access to this repository (the manuscript is the markdown; the PDF is built from it). Each reviewer has an assigned slice matched to their expertise, listed in your invitation and your tracking issue. Reading beyond your slice is welcome; the Introduction is assigned to everyone because it makes the book's promises.

## What feedback matters most, in order

1. **Technical truth.** A mechanism described wrongly, an incentive framed backwards, a number that doesn't recompute, a claim about Solana, Anchor, or Quasar that an expert would wince at. This is the feedback only you can give.
2. **What's missing.** A failure mode we don't warn about, a design consideration a practitioner would demand, a war story we should tell.
3. **What's misleading.** Technically-true sentences a newcomer would take the wrong way.
4. Prose style, typos, and layout are the least valuable use of your time; a copyeditor comes after you. Flag them if they jump out, but don't hunt.

Blunt is useful. "This section is wrong because X" is the most valuable sentence you can write us.

## How to file feedback

- **One GitHub issue per problem**, using the "Review feedback" template: page number (from your PDF), section heading, what's wrong, and (optional) what it should say. Small wording fixes are also welcome as direct PRs against the markdown.
- If you'd rather dump everything in one document or a marked-up PDF, that works too; send it to Mike and we'll split it into issues.
- Every correction that lands is credited in the Acknowledgements, and reviewers are named in About the Technical Reviewers (tell us if you'd rather not be).

## Timeline

Three weeks from your invitation. If that's tight, partial review of your assigned slice beats no review; tell Mike what you'll get to.
